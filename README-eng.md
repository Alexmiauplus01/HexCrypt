# CRYPTFALL

🇪🇸 *Versión en español: [README-esp.md](README-esp.md)*

A first-person roguelike with a pixel-art look in the style of *Daggerfall* / *Hexen*. It runs in your browser, nothing to install, and works offline once loaded. The language can be switched in the options.

**▶ Play:** https://alexmiauplus01.github.io/HexCrypt/

Delve into randomly generated multi-level dungeons, loot chests, find keys, face the Guardian, **get out alive with the loot** and grow ever stronger to take on tougher dungeons.

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Move |
| Mouse / Q E | Turn |
| Click / Space | Attack |
| R · C · V | Class skills |
| 1 2 3 / wheel | Switch weapon |
| I · J · M | Inventory · Journal · Map |
| H | Drink potion |
| F · Right click (hold) | Raise shield · Block / dodge |
| Shift | Dodge |
| P · N | Pause and options · Sound |

**With a controller** (Xbox, PlayStation and generic) or **on mobile** (landscape): joystick on the left, swipe on the right to turn, and buttons to attack, dodge, use skills, pause, journal, inventory and weapons.

## Gameplay

- **New game**: pick **Normal** or **Nightmare** mode (tougher, double gold and no common items), an optional **handicap** for more gold, and your class.
- **Auto-attack** (optional, in the options): with an enemy within 5 m, the camera aims at the nearest one by itself and attacks when it is in reach. If you attack or move the camera, you are in control; it starts aiming again 0.6 s after you let go.
- **Shield**: with one-handed weapons, `F` raises it. Hold right click (or the shield button on mobile) to **block** 80% of frontal damage; attacking while blocking does a **shield bash** that damages and pushes back. On mobile, drag from the shield button to walk while blocking.
- **Dodge**: an invulnerable dash in the direction you are moving. *Shadow Step* gives up to 3 charges.
- **Skills**: you carry 3 (`R` `C` `V`) and they level up to IV (more damage, shorter cooldown and something of their own: more projectiles, bigger areas, longer duration...). **Hold a skill for 2 s** and it turns **blue**: auto mode, it casts by itself whenever it is ready and enemies are in sight. Another 2 s switches it back to manual.
- **Guardian Souls**: every Guardian drops one; with them you evolve your class, learn its strongest skills and upgrade them. When you absorb it you pick an offering: its item, a skill upgrade, or shards and gold.
- **Potions**: they restore your full health (`H`) and, if you are about to die with one on you, you drink it automatically. They become scarce once you hoard 5 or more.
- **Enemies**: more of them show up the longer you stay in a room, and **waves** arrive every 2 minutes. They surround you, some telegraph their blows with a red circle, and there are elites and shamans. A red arc next to the crosshair shows where each hit comes from. They scale to you, so they always stay a challenge.
- **Loot**: six equipment slots (no backpack). Anything you don't want can be recycled for gold and shards, which the **blacksmith** uses to reforge. Rarity improves with every dungeon, and from the 3rd one on, dungeons go deeper and bring surprises.
- **Torches**: without light you only see the dim surroundings; each torch burns for 4 minutes.

You will find a **merchant** and a **blacksmith** in every dungeon, **vengeful ghosts** of your fallen characters and different bosses depending on the area, and the game saves by itself (CONTINUE on the title screen).

Walking into a wall opens doors, uses keys and stairs, and reveals secret passages.

<summary><b>🐄 Secret (spoiler)</b></summary>

After beating a dungeon's **Guardian**, input **up, up, down, down, left, right, left, right** and **attack** (with WASD, the arrow keys, the controller stick or the mobile joystick). A portal to **The Cursed Meadow** will open in front of you: a meadow beneath the dungeon full of **cow warriors** that come in herds and **cow mages** that hurl green orbs from afar.

At the far end awaits **THE COW KING**, who charges hard. Defeat him and he drops two boss treasures and a good handful of gold. There is one portal per dungeon, another portal takes you back to where you were, and the game does not save in the meadow.
